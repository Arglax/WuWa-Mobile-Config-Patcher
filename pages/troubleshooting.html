Troubleshooting & Common Issues

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Shizuku is Running, but App Shows "NONE" or Requests Access

If your Elevated Access Status shows "NONE" despite Shizuku running in the background[cite: 9]:

  1. Go to the Settings tab and check the Elevated Access Status card[cite: 9].
  2. Tap the refresh button next to the Shizuku setup wizard[cite: 9].
  3. If it fails, open the Shizuku app, revoke permissions for WuWa Config Patcher, and force stop the patcher. Reopen the patcher to trigger the prompt again via rikka.shizuku.api[cite: 9].

2. The Game is Stuttering / Crashing After a Patch

Not all devices handle extreme presets well. To safely reset your game graphics to Kuro Games' defaults[cite: 9]:

  1. Navigate to the Utilities tab $\rightarrow$ Common Subtab[cite: 9].
  2. Tap the primary red Revert to Vanilla button[cite: 9].
  3. Confirm the prompt. This automatically executes a safe deletion of Engine.ini, DeviceProfiles.ini, and Scalability.ini[cite: 9].

3. What do the Access Badges Mean?

  • Green Badge (SHIZUKU): Primary wireless debugging backend[cite: 9].
  • Red Badge (ROOT): Non-interactive su -c id backend for rooted devices[cite: 9].
  • Blue Badge (AXMANAGER): Axeron community provider helper[cite: 9].

4. Editor Saves are Flagged in Vibrant Red

The Config Editor enforces strict CVar Section Guards. Misplaced lines are painted #FF2222[cite: 9].

  1. When you tap Save, choose Auto-Fix Sections[cite: 9].
  2. The app will automatically route your CVars to canonical headers (e.g., s. commands to [/Script/Engine.StreamingSettings])[cite: 9].
  3. Note: [Core.Paths], [Core.System], and [/Script/Engine.RendererOverrideSettings] are whitelisted and will never be altered by the Auto-Fix tool[cite: 9].

5. Device Storage is Full from Logs

Wuthering Waves can generate massive log files over time. To clean them[cite: 9]:

  1. Go to Utilities $\rightarrow$ Log Tools[cite: 9].
  2. Tap Delete Logs to execute an elevated rm -rf on the .../Saved/Logs/ directory safely[cite: 9].
